# Software &amp; Tools

We release code, simulators, and toolkits so that others can build on our work. A selection:

* **[PedSUMO](https://github.com/M-Colley/pedsumo)** — open-source tooling to study large-scale effects
  of external Human-Machine Interfaces (eHMIs) and automated-vehicle behaviour in traffic simulation
  (built on SUMO).
* **[Bayesian Optimization for Unity](https://github.com/Pascal-Jansen/Bayesian-Optimization-for-Unity)**
  — a human-in-the-loop multi-objective Bayesian optimisation toolkit for interactive systems, used in
  our OptiCarVis and eHMI optimisation work.
* **[Causal Models (DAGs)](https://m-colley.github.io/dagitty/dags.html)** — directed acyclic graphs
  documenting causal assumptions behind our studies.

For more, see the group's GitHub: **[github.com/M-Colley](https://github.com/M-Colley)**.
